1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VaultVid: Video Downloader handles information when you use the Android app. The app is developed by [LEGAL DEVELOPER OR COMPANY NAME] and is intended to provide on-device media download and media-management features.

VaultVid is an on-device media downloader and media-management utility. It allows users to paste a media URL and inspect compatible video or audio formats; search the web through DuckDuckGo; open websites in an in-app browser; detect direct MP4 video URLs and HLS/m3u8 media streams; download media that the user owns or is authorized to save; manage background downloads, progress, speed, and download queues; save completed media to device storage; use optional website cookies for media the user is authorized to access; play downloaded videos; decode password-protected VaultVid video files locally; and configure download folders, appearance, concurrency, and media-processing options.

VaultVid does not bypass DRM, paywalls, authentication requirements, or access controls. YouTube, Google Video, known adult domains, and other restricted services are intentionally unsupported or blocked.

3. Current data practices

The current release:

  • Does not require a VaultVid account.
  • Does not operate a developer-controlled backend for user data.
  • Does not include advertising SDKs.
  • Does not include analytics SDKs.
  • Does not include developer-controlled crash-reporting services.
  • Does not sell personal data.
  • Does not use downloaded media for advertising or profiling.
  • Does not upload a user’s media library to the developer.
  • Processes app preferences, download queues, and media operations locally on the device.

4. Data processed locally

VaultVid may access or process the following information locally to provide requested features:

  1. URLs pasted or entered by the user.
  2. Search terms entered for DuckDuckGo searches.
  3. Current page URLs and detected media-stream URLs.
  4. Download metadata, progress, history, file names, and output locations.
  5. App settings, appearance, download directory, and concurrency preferences.
  6. Cookies created through an optional user-initiated third-party website login.
  7. Media files selected, downloaded, played, encrypted, or decoded by the user.
  8. Passwords/passphrases entered to decode VaultVid encrypted files.

VaultVid may store and use this information on the device to complete user-requested operations. Decode passphrases are used only for the requested on-device operation and are not intentionally retained or transmitted to a VaultVid server.

5. Third-party websites and network requests

When users search, browse, sign in, inspect a URL, or download media, the device communicates directly with DuckDuckGo or the website/media server selected by the user.

Those third parties may receive the user’s IP address, user-agent, request headers, requested URL, cookies, and other technically necessary connection information. Third-party websites process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. VaultVid does not control third-party website content or privacy practices. Users should review a website’s privacy policy before signing in or submitting information.

Optional cookies may be sent back to the relevant website to perform a user-authorized request. VaultVid does not claim affiliation with DuckDuckGo or any website opened by the user.

VaultVid does not operate a developer-controlled backend for user media, download history, or personal account data. Media and network communication are handled between the app and the chosen website or media server, as initiated by the user.

6. Device permissions

VaultVid requests Android permissions only to provide user-requested functionality. The permissions and their limited purposes are:

  • INTERNET: search websites, inspect media sources, and download requested media.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: display download progress and completion notifications.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: continue active downloads while the app is in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK: prevent an active transfer from being interrupted while necessary.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 9 and older: save or access media at the user’s request.
  • File and folder access selected by the user: choose encrypted files, output files, and download locations.

These permissions should only be used to provide user-requested functionality and should not be used for unrelated data collection.

7. Data retention and deletion

VaultVid stores some information locally, as follows:

  • Preferences, cookies, queues, and temporary data remain on the device until removed by the user, cleared through Android settings, or removed by uninstalling the app.
  • Downloaded or exported files stored in shared storage may remain after the app is uninstalled.
  • Users must delete shared media files manually using VaultVid, Android settings, or a file manager.
  • Because VaultVid has no account or developer-controlled user database, there is no remote account-deletion process for the current release.
  • Users may contact longnpdevmobile@gmail.com with privacy questions.
  • If future versions introduce accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, or a backend, the policy and Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before those features are released.
